Lead engineer Elena García asked the operator to close valve C, reduce pump speed to 1,450 revolutions per minute, and wait ninety seconds before taking another reading.

The alert was serious but not an emergency. “Stay calm, follow the checklist, and confirm each step aloud,” Elena said.
